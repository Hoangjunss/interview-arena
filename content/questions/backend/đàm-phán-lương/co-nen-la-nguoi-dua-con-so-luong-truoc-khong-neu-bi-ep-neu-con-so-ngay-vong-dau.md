## Câu hỏi (VI)
Có nên là người đưa con số lương trước không? Nếu bị ép nêu con số ngay vòng đầu thì xử lý thế nào?

## Question (EN)
Should you name a salary number first? What if you are pushed for one in the first round?

## Đáp án chi tiết (VI)
$7a

## Detailed Answer (EN)
General rule: **let the company state its band first**, since they already have a budget. In Vietnam, though, HR usually asks for expectations during screening to filter fast, so dodging indefinitely just drags the process out.\
\
**Handle it in this order:**\
1. **Ask back first:** \\"Do you have a band budgeted for this role? I would like to check alignment before we both invest more time.\\"\
2. If they push it back to you, **give a range** rather than a point: \\"Around A–B million gross, depending on scope and benefits.\\"\
3. Set the **bottom of the range at the number you would genuinely be happy with**, not your absolute minimum — offers land near the bottom of whatever you state.\
\
**Prepare three numbers before the interview:** your target, your walk-away floor, and the market rate for your level (from IT salary reports and comparable postings). Without them every answer is guesswork.\
\
Avoid \\"anything is fine, I care about learning\\" — that reliably produces an offer at the bottom of the budget.
